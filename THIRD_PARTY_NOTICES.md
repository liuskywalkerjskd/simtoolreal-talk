Paper figures in `assets/fig*.png` are taken from Kedia, Lum, Bohg and Liu, *SimToolReal: An Object-Centric Policy for Zero-Shot Dexterous Tool Manipulation*, arXiv:2602.16863v2. Videos and their poster frames come from https://simtoolreal.github.io/. Exact URLs and transformations are documented in `assets/videos/README.md`. These are author-provided demonstrations. They are not simulations or recordings made by the presenter. All original authors retain rights to their materials. Inclusion here is for an attributed academic seminar; no blanket redistribution license is asserted.
